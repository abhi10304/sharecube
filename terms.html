4. Driving safety

Never watch video while driving. Car Screen is for passengers, and for when the car is parked. Watching video while driving is dangerous and against the law in many countries.

  • Video on the car screen pauses automatically while the car is moving. There is no way to turn this off.
  • The lock depends on your phone's motion or location readings, which can be wrong or late. You, the driver, are always responsible for driving safely and obeying traffic laws.
  • Don't handle your phone while driving. Set things up before you start.
