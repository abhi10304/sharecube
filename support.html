How do I open this on my TV?

Pick your TV. Then type the address your phone shows.

Samsung
  1. Press Home on the remote and open the Internet app.
  2. Select the address bar at the top, not the search box.
  3. Type the address from your phone, then press Done.
LG
  1. Press Home on the remote and open Web Browser.
  2. Select the address bar at the top.
  3. Type the address from your phone, then press Enter.
Android TV / Google TV (Mi, OnePlus, TCL, Vu, Realme, Sony, Philips, Airtel)
  1. Install the free ShareCube TV app from Google Play on the TV.
  2. Open ShareCube TV, pick this phone, and type the code if asked.
  3. No app? Install TV Bro from Google Play (not Puffin: it can't reach your phone) and type the address.

Tip: Typing with the remote is slow. Type from your phone instead: Google TV app › Remote › keyboard.

Get the free ShareCube TV app

Fire TV
  1. Install the free ShareCube TV app from the Amazon Appstore.
  2. Open ShareCube TV, pick this phone, and type the code if asked.
  3. Or open the Silk browser and type the address from your phone.

Tip: Newer Fire TV Sticks with Vega OS can't install Android apps. On those, use the Silk browser.

Get the free ShareCube TV app

Jio Set-Top Box
  1. Open the JioPages browser on the set-top box.
  2. Select the address bar at the top.
  3. Type the address from your phone, then press OK.
Laptop or computer
  1. Open Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  2. Click the address bar at the top.
  3. Type the address from your phone and press Enter.

Tips for typing on a TV

  • Use the number keys on the remote for the numbers.
  • Type in the address bar, not a search box.
  • If the TV searches Google instead, add http:// at the start.
  • Bookmark the page once. Next time it opens with no code.

Frequently asked questions

Mirror or Car Screen: which one do I need?

Both are in the Screen tab.

  • Mirror copies your whole phone screen and its sound, live, to a TV, laptop or car browser. Use it for photos, apps, games and presentations. Your phone has to stay on and unlocked, because it's what the screen shows.
  • Car Screen sends a video or a website to a car or TV that has its own web browser. The car's browser plays it from the internet itself, and your phone is only the remote, so you can lock your phone. It's best for watching videos in the car while parked.

If your car's screen can't open websites at all, it can't use either one. ShareCube doesn't use CarPlay or Android Auto, which don't allow browsers or video.

How do I mirror my phone to my TV?
  1. Put your phone and TV on the same Wi‑Fi.
  2. In ShareCube, open Screen › Mirror, tap Start Mirroring and confirm.
  3. Open the web browser on your TV and type the address shown in ShareCube. Not sure where the browser is? See How do I open this on my TV?

No app is needed on the TV. It works with most smart TVs from 2018 onward, and with laptops, tablets and car screens that have a web browser. On Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV, the free ShareCube TV app is the easiest way: pick your phone, no typing.

Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar and most paid streaming apps block screen sharing, so the TV shows black. That's their rule, not a fault. Use the TV's own app for those. YouTube, photos, games and most apps work.

Can I start without opening the app?

iPhone: open Control Center, touch and hold Screen Recording, choose ShareCube, then tap Start Broadcast. You can also ask Siri to "Start mirroring with ShareCube", and add ShareCube's Hide Screen and Mirror controls to Control Center (iOS 18 and later).

Android: add the Mirror screen, Transfer and Car Screen tiles to Quick Settings, add the ShareCube widget to your home screen, or long-press the app icon for shortcuts.

How do I use it in my car?

Your car needs a screen with a web browser (many cars with a built-in browser, and car media boxes). Turn on your phone's hotspot and connect the car to it, or put both on the car's Wi‑Fi. Then use Car Screen for videos, or Mirror for your whole screen, and open the address in the car's browser. Turn on Keep the Same Code so you can bookmark the link once. On iPhone, the hotspot address is always 172.20.10.1. Most cars allow their browser only while parked, and ShareCube pauses video by itself while the car moves.

How do I show a video or a website on my car screen?

Open Screen › Car Screen and tap Start Car Screen. In your car's (or TV's) web browser, type the address shown, or scan the QR code. The car shows a launcher with big tiles. Then open a video or website in ShareCube's browser and tap Send to Car. On Android you can also use Send to Car from any app's share menu.

Your phone is the remote: play, pause, skip 10 seconds, volume, next and previous. You can lock your phone. On iPhone, the Lock Screen, the Dynamic Island and an Apple Watch keep the controls. On Android, the lock screen, the notification and a paired watch do. The car screen has its own big buttons too.

The car's browser loads the video or website from the internet itself. Your phone only sends the address and remote commands.

Why does video pause while I'm driving?

Car Screen is for passengers, and for when the car is parked. Watching video while driving is dangerous and against the law in many countries, and the driver can see the car's screen. So video pauses by itself while the car moves, and there's no way to turn this off. Never watch video while driving.

  • Your phone senses the motion: Motion & Fitness on iPhone, GPS speed on Android. The readings stay on your phone. Nothing is stored or sent.
  • The car shows "Paused while driving". When the car stops, tap Play on the car.
  • If you don't allow motion or location, the car asks "Is the car parked?" before each video and every 10 minutes.
  • For sound on the move, use YouTube Music on your phone (through CarPlay or Android Auto), or start a Party with your own music. YouTube doesn't allow its sound to play without the video.
Does ShareCube work offline, without internet?

Yes. ShareCube never needs the internet for your screen, files, camera, music or voice: they go directly between your devices. The devices just need to share a network:

  • Mirror, Transfer and Live Camera work on any Wi‑Fi with no internet (a travel router, a home network that's down), or with the other device on your phone's hotspot.
  • Party Speaker and Walkie-Talkie between iPhones need no network at all. Keep Wi‑Fi turned on, and the iPhones connect directly.
  • With Android phones, Party Speaker and Walkie-Talkie need the same Wi‑Fi or one phone's hotspot. Android's Fast Link makes its own Wi‑Fi for Transfer.
  • Car Screen is the exception: the videos and websites come from the internet, through the car's own connection or your hotspot.

When your phone has no network, ShareCube says "You're offline" and shows what still works.

What is Fast Link? (Android)

Fast Link is the quickest way to move files between an Android phone and a computer. Your phone makes its own small Wi‑Fi network, and the computer joins it, so files go straight through the air with no router in between.

  1. Open Transfer › Share Files and tap Start Fast Link.
  2. On the computer, choose the network name shown and type the password (on a phone, scan the code).
  3. Open the address shown in the computer's browser and enter the code.

That Wi‑Fi has no internet, so the computer is offline while it's joined. Android 13 and later asks to allow "Nearby devices" for this. Android 10 to 12 asks for location permission, because those versions require it to make a Wi‑Fi network; ShareCube never reads your location. Fast Link can't start while your phone's own hotspot is on.

Can iPhones and Android phones use ShareCube together?

Yes.

  • Party Speaker and Walkie-Talkie work between iPhones and Android phones with ShareCube, as long as they're on the same Wi‑Fi or one phone's hotspot. The no-Wi‑Fi direct link works only between iPhones. Please update ShareCube on every phone: older iPhone versions can't be joined by Android phones.
  • Transfer works with any phone that has a web browser: open the address shown, and send or receive files.
  • Mirror, Live Camera and Car Screen show on any browser, whichever phone you use.
How do I send photos to an Android phone or a PC?

Open the Transfer tab, choose Share Files, add photos or files, and tap Start Sharing. On the other device, scan the QR code or type the address into its browser. It can download your files one by one or as a ZIP, and send files back. A live speed meter shows how fast it's going. Both devices need to be on the same Wi‑Fi, or the other device on your phone's hotspot (or Fast Link on Android).

How do I back up my photos to a PC without the cloud?

Open Transfer › Photos Backup and allow access to your photos. Open the address on your computer and choose Back Up Everything New. Photos and videos download at full quality in ZIP files, and next time only new ones are included. Keep ShareCube open until it finishes, check the ZIP is saved, then choose Mark as Backed Up.

How do I scan a document to my computer?

Open Transfer › Scan to PC and scan the pages. The PDF appears straight away on the computer's browser page. On iPhone, ShareCube reads the text on the phone, so the PDF is searchable and comes with a text file. On Android, take a photo of each page (or add photos you already have), put them in order and tap Save: the pages are saved as pictures in one PDF, made entirely on the phone, so its text can't be searched.

Can I use my phone as a webcam or baby monitor?

Yes. In the Camera tab, choose Webcam, Baby Monitor or Home Camera and tap Start Camera, then open the address on your laptop or another device. To use it in a video call, share that browser window. Keep ShareCube open on the phone, because the system pauses the camera when you switch apps. Smart Alerts show on the watching browser: loud sound and motion on both, and crying on iPhone. ShareCube isn't a medical device.

Sound is off unless you turn it on, and the watching browser shows LIVE while the camera is on. Only film people who agree. Some places require everyone's consent to record sound.

How do I make several phones play music together?

On one phone, open Party & Talk › Speaker, choose where the sound comes from and tap Start Party. Friends scan the QR code with their Camera app, or open ShareCube › Party & Talk › Speaker and tap your party. Laptops can listen in a browser through the second QR code. Between iPhones no Wi‑Fi network is needed. With Android phones or browsers, use the same Wi‑Fi or a hotspot.

Why can't I pick a song from Apple Music? Songs from Apple Music are protected, so apps can't read them as files. On iPhone, host with Any App instead, then play the song in Apple Music.

How far does Walkie-Talkie reach?

Walkie-Talkie is in Party & Talk › Walkie-Talkie. It reaches about as far as Wi‑Fi: usually a house, a campsite, or cars driving close together. Between iPhones no Wi‑Fi network or internet is needed. With Android phones, everyone needs the same Wi‑Fi or one phone's hotspot. If someone goes out of range, they reconnect automatically when they come back.

How do I block or report someone in Walkie-Talkie?

Tap the … next to the person, or touch and hold their message. Block hides their messages and photos and mutes their voice on your phone. The channel's creator can also Remove them. Walkie-Talkie has no server, so messages exist only on the phones in the channel and disappear when it ends. To tell us about abuse, email us with the channel name and the time. If you feel unsafe, contact local emergency services.

Can other people see my screen?

Only screens on your own network that enter your code. Wrong codes are blocked after a few tries, and the stream never goes to the internet. Mirroring shows everything on your screen, including messages from other people. To keep notifications off the TV, turn on Do Not Disturb before you start, or use Hide Screen whenever you need a moment of privacy.

What does "Data Not Collected" mean?

The App Store and Google Play listings say Data Not Collected. That's because ShareCube works only between your own devices:

  • Your screen, files, camera, voice and messages go straight from your phone to the other device on your network. We run no servers for them, so we never see them.
  • There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Things like the driving lock and the Walkie-Talkie word filter work on your phone only.
  • We only get an email if you choose to send one, for example a support question or a Walkie-Talkie report.

Troubleshooting

The TV page says it can't connect
  • Check both devices are on the same Wi‑Fi, and not a guest network (guest networks often block devices from seeing each other).
  • Type the address in the browser's address bar, not a search box. If the TV searches Google, add http:// at the start.
  • Turn off any VPN on your phone.
  • Still stuck? Turn on your phone's hotspot and connect the TV to it. Mirroring uses no mobile data.
  • On iPhone, check that Local Network is on: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › ShareCube.
  • Type the address exactly as shown, including the port number and the code.
  • Keep ShareCube open on screen for Transfer and Camera, because the system pauses them in the background.
The TV shows a black screen

Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar and most paid streaming apps block screen sharing, so the TV shows black. That's their rule, not a fault. Use the TV's own app for those. YouTube, photos, games and most apps work.

  1. Check Hide Screen is off in ShareCube.
  2. Open a photo or YouTube on your phone to test the picture.
  3. Old TV browser? Use the free ShareCube TV app, TV Bro or Silk instead.
Where did received files go?
  • iPhone: open the Files app › On My iPhone › ShareCube.
  • Android: open Files or Downloads › ShareCube.
The TV browser can't play the video

ShareCube needs a browser that can play live video. Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox work, as do most smart TVs from 2018 onward. Updating the TV's software often helps.

The picture is choppy

Choose Smooth under Picture quality, move closer to your Wi‑Fi router, or use 5 GHz Wi‑Fi.

There's no sound on the TV
  1. Check Sound on TV is on in ShareCube, and the TV isn't muted.
  2. Some TV browsers need one press first: select Turn on sound on the TV page.
  3. On Android, allow "Record audio". It's how Android sends your apps' sound; the microphone isn't used.

Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar and most paid streaming apps block screen sharing, so the TV shows black. That's their rule, not a fault. Use the TV's own app for those. YouTube, photos, games and most apps work.

The Car Screen remote stopped working

What's playing on the car keeps playing, and the car screen's own buttons always work. On iPhone, iOS pauses ShareCube in the background: use the Car Screen controls on the Lock Screen (they reconnect within a few seconds), or open ShareCube again. On Android, Car Screen keeps running with its notification. If the car left the ShareCube page after you opened a tile or website, open your ShareCube bookmark to come back.

Party or Walkie-Talkie can't find other phones

Turn on Wi‑Fi on every phone and allow Local Network for ShareCube on iPhone. iPhones don't need to join a network. With an Android phone in the group, everyone must be on the same Wi‑Fi or one phone's hotspot, and every phone needs the latest ShareCube. Keep phones within Wi‑Fi range, or join by typing the 6-character code. If the sound is out of step with a Bluetooth speaker, use Fine-Tune on the listening phone.

Android asks about sharing my screen every time

That's expected. Android asks you to confirm each time screen sharing starts. Choose to share your entire screen. You can stop from the ShareCube notification, the Mirror screen tile, or Android's screen-sharing indicator.

Availability

ShareCube is on iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android (Android 10 or later), with the same five tabs: Home, Screen (Mirror and Car Screen), Transfer, Camera and Party & Talk (Party Speaker and Walkie-Talkie). A few things differ. Only iPhone has baby-crying alerts, searchable scans with scan summaries, Party Speaker's "Any App" sound and the no-Wi‑Fi direct link between phones. Only Android has Fast Link and "Send to Car" from any app's share menu. Some features need a newer system: Control Center controls need iOS 18, and the Apple Watch car controls need iOS 18 and watchOS 11.
