ShareCube

Your phone on any TV, laptop or car screen that has a web browser, with nothing to install on the other screen. For iPhone and Android. No account, no ads and no tracking. Everything goes straight between your own devices.

Six things, one app

  • MirrorYour whole screen and sound, live, on a TV, laptop or car browser. Hide Screen in one tap.
  • Car ScreenSend a video or website to a car or TV with its own web browser. Your phone is the remote, even when it's locked. Video pauses by itself while the car moves.
  • TransferSend and receive files with any computer or phone, back up your photos without the cloud, scan documents to PDFs (searchable on iPhone), and share the clipboard. On Android, Fast Link makes its own Wi‑Fi for the fastest transfers.
  • Live CameraA webcam, baby monitor or home camera in any browser, with sound and motion alerts detected on the phone.
  • Party SpeakerSeveral phones play the same music in sync, like one big speaker. Laptops can listen too.
  • Walkie-TalkieHold to talk and chat with phones nearby, with no internet. Works between iPhones and Android phones on the same Wi‑Fi or a hotspot.

Also: widgets, Live Activities, Control Center controls and Siri on iPhone; Quick Settings tiles, a widget and shortcuts on Android. ShareCube doesn't use CarPlay or Android Auto: Car Screen needs a car with a web browser.
