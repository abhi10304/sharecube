3. What we collect: "Data Not Collected"

The App Store and Google Play listings say Data Not Collected. Here is what that means.

  • Everything happens on your devices. When you use a feature, your phone talks directly to your other device on your local network. The data never passes through a server of ours. We have no such server, so we can't see your screen, files, camera, voice or messages.
  • No account, analytics, ads or tracking. The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no advertising. It contains no other company's analytics or data-collecting code. On iPhone it uses only Apple's frameworks. On Android it uses only the Android system, the standard AndroidX and Kotlin libraries, and the open-source ZXing library to read QR codes on the phone, with no Google Play services or Firebase.
  • Processing on your phone isn't collection. Apple and Google both say data that stays on your device, or goes only between your own devices, is not "collected". That's why the labels say Data Not Collected.

The only way personal data reaches us is an email you choose to send. See section 4.

4. Emails you send us

5. How the app works: your devices, your network

When you use a ShareCube feature, your phone runs a small web server that only devices on your local network can reach: the same Wi‑Fi, a phone's hotspot, Android's Fast Link or Car Wi‑Fi, a direct iPhone-to-iPhone link, or, for Party Speaker on Android, Bluetooth between nearby phones (Car Link, for a car where the phones share no Wi‑Fi). The data goes straight between the two devices:

  • Mirror: your screen and app sound go to the TV, computer or car browser you choose.
  • Car Screen: web addresses, remote-control commands and text you type with Type on Car go from your phone to your car's or TV's browser.
  • Live Camera: the live picture, and sound only if you turn it on, go to the browser you choose.
  • Transfer: files, photos, scans and clipboard text go between your phone and the computer or phone you choose.
  • Party Speaker: music or sound goes from the host phone to listening phones and browsers nearby, over the local network or Bluetooth. A phone that asks to join without the code shows its own name (as set in its settings) on the host's phone, so the host can tap Allow. A phone the host lets in gets a random key, kept on both phones only, so it can join that host's later parties without the code; the host can forget these phones at any time, and unused keys are forgotten after 90 days.
  • Walkie-Talkie: voice, text and photos go between the phones in a channel.
  • ShareCube TV: the TV app finds phones running ShareCube on the same network and shows their stream. It remembers the last phone's name and address on the TV only, and sends nothing to us.

Each session is protected by a random code, and connections from outside your local network are refused. Party Speaker and Walkie-Talkie connections between phones are encrypted with keys only those phones have, over Wi‑Fi and over Bluetooth alike. Other local connections rely on your Wi‑Fi's own encryption (WPA2/WPA3).

Bluetooth (Car Link). While you host a party on Android with Bluetooth allowed, phones within Bluetooth range can see that a party is on and its name, as phones on the same Wi‑Fi can; joining needs the code or your Allow, and the music is encrypted. A phone looking for parties only looks for ShareCube's own Bluetooth signal and reads the party's name from it. Nothing goes through us or the internet, and Bluetooth is never used to find your location.

6. Data kept on your device

  • Settings: picture quality, launcher tiles, your Walkie-Talkie name and avatar, the people you blocked, and whether you accepted the Walkie-Talkie rules and the Live Camera notice.
  • Pairing tokens ("remembered screens"): after a TV, computer or car enters a code once, your phone keeps a one-way fingerprint of a random key that browser holds, so it can reconnect without the code. Forget Screens deletes them, and one unused for 90 days is deleted by itself. On Android they're left out of cloud backup.
  • Files and scans you receive stay in the app's storage until you delete them or delete the app. On Android, "Save to Downloads" copies a file only when you choose it.
  • Photos Backup remembers which photos each computer already has, on your phone only. "Forget This Computer" removes it.
  • Walkie-Talkie chat and clipboard history live in memory only, and disappear when the channel or session ends.
  • Live Camera records nothing, unless you turn on Save Motion Clips (iPhone), which saves short clips to your own photo library.
  • Widgets, Live Activities, tiles and notifications are made on your phone. No push server is used.

7. Location and motion: the driving lock

Car Screen pauses video while the car is moving, so the driver isn't watching video. To know if the car is moving:

  • iPhone uses Motion & Fitness (it says when you're in a moving vehicle).
  • Android uses the GPS speed from precise location, only while Car Screen is on.

These readings are used on your phone only. They are never stored and never sent to us or anyone else. Only "moving" or "stopped" goes to the car's page. If you don't allow it, the car asks "Is the car parked?" before each video instead.

9. On-device processing

  • Scan to PC reads text on iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. Android makes image PDFs on the phone.
  • Scan summaries (iPhone) use Apple Intelligence's on-device model where available. The scan isn't sent to Apple or to us.
  • Sound and motion alerts in Live Camera are detected on the phone. Nothing is recorded or uploaded to detect them.
  • The Walkie-Talkie word filter hides offensive words on each phone, from a list built into the app.
  • Walkie-Talkie photos are shrunk, and their location and camera details removed, before they're sent.

10. Websites, YouTube and Car Screen

Car Screen includes ShareCube's own browser. When you open a website, your phone loads it from the internet. When you send a video or website to your car or TV, the car's or TV's browser loads it from the internet itself.

Car Screen uses YouTube API Services. YouTube videos play in YouTube's own embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com). By playing YouTube videos you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Websites and services you open, including YouTube, are run by others and may collect information under their own policies. ShareCube adds no tracking to them, and we don't receive anything about what you browse.

13. Children and age

ShareCube isn't made for children. It's rated 16+ on the App Store, because the in-app browser can open any website and Walkie-Talkie lets nearby people chat. 14. Security

Every session uses a random 6-character code. Wrong codes are locked out after a few tries, and only devices on your local network can connect. You can end any session at any time, and Hide Screen stops sending your picture and sound at once. To report a security problem, email elyxaj@gmail.com.
